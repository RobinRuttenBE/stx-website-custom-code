What the exam asks of you

Two parts, an hour and a half each, 100 points in total. You need 85 to pass.

Part one 30 points 1 h 30

Free creativity

Your own designs, made with your own Sempertex balloons. Every design uses at least three different balloon sizes.

    Part two 70 points 1 h 30

    Decoration on the jury's brief

    The jury sets the assignment on the day. Sempertex supplies the balloons for this part, so everyone works with the same material.
