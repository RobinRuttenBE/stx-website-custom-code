Let's see if we're a good fit. Here is how it works.

We believe in building strong, long-term partnerships. That's why we like to make sure there's a good fit on both sides from the start.

1

Apply

Fill in the two minute form above and pick a time slot for your intake call.

2

Intake call

A call of 30 to 60 minutes with our team. We look at your business, your region and your customers, and check honestly whether we can serve your business well and whether it is the right match on both sides.

3

Partnership and launch

If it is a match, you become an official partner. You receive your starter package with launch support, ready to sell the week it arrives.

✓

Zero dead stock risk on your first order*

Here is exactly how it works. You place your first order. Anything that has not sold after 90 days, we buy back or swap for products that fit your customers better. Your money back, or a shelf that sells. Either way, your first order cannot leave you stuck with stock. You test Sempertex in your shop, we carry the risk.
