Number stack

Foil numbers on a base of quads. Single digit: three quads stacked like a column. Two or more digits: quads on their side, tied in a row. Every foil stands in a tie point between the balloons, never on top of one. On the shop every digit is the real Grabo foil, with its photo, on the order list.
