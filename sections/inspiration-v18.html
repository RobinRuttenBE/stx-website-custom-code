Your rates

Add up everything you pay every month, even in a month without a single job: van or car, insurance, storage or studio rent, software, phone, website, accountant. Fill in that monthly total, plus the hours you spend on jobs in a normal month. The tool turns that into an overhead rate per hour. A pillar of 20 minutes carries a small share, a full day of inflating and installing carries a big one.

Example: van 150 + insurance 60 + storage 120 + software 30 + phone 40 = 400 per month. With 40 hours on jobs a month that is 10 per hour. A job of 4 hours carries 40, a job of 1 hour carries 10.
